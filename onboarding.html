Independent Cleaning Professional

One:18 Cleaning is a growing residential and property cleaning company serving the Greater Houston area. Inspired by Isaiah 1:18, our brand is built around integrity, excellence, service, and attention to detail.

Types of Cleaning Opportunities

Recurring residential cleaning · Deep cleaning · Move-in / move-out cleaning · Realtor and listing-preparation cleaning · Airbnb / short-term rental turnovers · Post-construction and builder final cleaning · Apartment and rental-property turnovers · Select commercial cleaning opportunities

What We're Looking For

Dependable and punctual · Detail-oriented · Professional and respectful in clients' homes · Experienced in residential or professional cleaning · Comfortable working independently · Able to communicate clearly and professionally · Committed to delivering high-quality results · Able to reliably travel to accepted projects throughout their selected Greater Houston service area

Contractor Requirements

Operate as an independent contractor and complete required 1099/W-9 documentation · Provide valid identification · Successfully complete applicable screening/background-check requirements · Maintain reliable transportation · Have a reliable smartphone for project information and communication · Maintain any required business or liability insurance applicable to assigned work · Provide cleaning supplies/equipment when required for the particular contracted project

Compensation

Competitive project-based compensation, varying by property size, cleaning type, scope of work, project complexity, location, and approved add-on services. Additional opportunities may become available for cleaning professionals who consistently receive strong quality feedback and demonstrate reliability.

This posting describes opportunities for independent contractors, not guaranteed employment, hours, or project volume. Contractors may accept or decline offered projects. Final project terms and compensation are disclosed before acceptance.
